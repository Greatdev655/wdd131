    Lagos, Nigeria

    official Nigerian Flag

    Nigeria is often called the “Giant of Africa,” and for good reason. With over 200 million people, it is the most populous country on the continent. Located in West Africa, Nigeria is known for its rich cultural diversity, natural resources, and growing economy. From bustling cities to beautiful landscapes, Nigeria offers a unique blend of tradition and modern development. Nigeria is home to more than 250 ethnic groups, each with its own language, customs, and traditions. The three largest ethnic groups are the Hausa-Fulani in the north, the Yoruba in the southwest, and the Igbo in the southeast. English is the official language, but many local languages are spoken across the country.
